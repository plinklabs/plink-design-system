Instrument-panel surface: flat fill + 1px hairline border, never a drop shadow.

```jsx
<Card num="01">
  <h3>Timer</h3>
  <p>A big, calm classroom countdown.</p>
</Card>
<Card variant="ink" interactive>Dark panel</Card>
```

Variants: `default` (paper) · `raised` · `ink` (full-bleed dark). `interactive` adds a hairline hover; `num` shows a mono section number.
